SPI General Ledger (GL) Module — REPORTS
Source: Sidat Hyder Financials Software Guide

### Reports 

The reports window has the following setups:

[Chart of accounts:]{.underline}

As the title implies, this report is used to print the Chart of Accounts
of a company or companies if the multiple companies' option is being
used.

[Getting there:]{.underline}

On the main menu screen go to the Reports menu and then select *Chart of
Accounts*.

Click the option that takes you to the printing screen. There are
various criteria available that allow you to print the Chart of Accounts
for the given range of account codes.

You may sort the report on account code, title creation date etc.

If required, apply various available filters to generate the report
based on required criteria.

[Transaction Listing:]{.underline}

As the title implies, the selected report displays a summary of
transactions for any given account.

[Getting there]{.underline}:

On the main menu screen go to the Reports menu and then select
*Transaction*

*Listing*.

Click the option that takes you to the printing screen that has various
criteria facilitating you to generate a list of transactions that have
been entered into the system.

You can send a request to the system to generate the report for all
voucher types or a specific voucher type.

The generated list may contain all vouchers for the above specification
or a range of vouchers falling within a certain period or between two
given periods

You can also specify a listing of these vouchers based on being entered
between a specific range of dates or even modified between a specific
range of dates irrespective of entry dates. Various such criteria are
available.

As you will see on the screen you are also allowed to list these
vouchers in various reporting currencies (if the multi currency option
has been enabled) which means you can generate a listing of these
vouchers entered in currency A but reported in currency B with
appropriate reflection of conversion rates.

In addition the selected report may also be printed in DOS mode or if
require saved in a text file.

If you are using multiple sites then you are also provided the option to
view consolidated data for all the sites or a combination of sites or
simply one site. Just select from the available options to view the
results.

[Withholding tax reports:]{.underline}

As the title implies, this options contains various reports that are
related to numerous withholding tax reports, many of them meeting
specific standards meeting governmental requirements.

[Getting there:]{.underline}

On the main menu screen go to the Reports menu and then select
*Withholding Tax Reports* that contains various sub-options discussed
below:

[Tax payment receipt setup:]{.underline}

As the title implies, this options allows you to define and setup your
*Tax Payment Receipt*.

[Getting there:]{.underline}

On the main menu screen go to the Reports menu and then select sub-menu
item [Tax Payment Receipt]{.underline} within menu item Withholding Tax
Reports.

Click the option that takes you to the setup screen that has various
criteria facilitating you to define your own printing specifications for
the tax receipt. The screen contains various setup fields manipulating
the options for which, allow you to define the format of the challan for
subsequent printing on pre-printed stationery.

Choose to include or exclude fields and place them on specific positions
by using XY Coordinates.

You can also adjust the Font size and width as according to your
requirement.

**Format...** Clicking this button will display another window where you
can manually adjust the Tax Payment Receipt by using the mouse to drag
and drop fields. In order to identify a field, simply click the right
mouse button on an item to display a tool tip like help informing you
about the nature of the field.

[Tax payment receipt challan:]{.underline}

As the title implies, this report print the [Tax Payment Receipt
(Challan)]{.underline}

[Getting there:]{.underline}

On the main menu screen go to the Reports menu and then select sub-menu
item [Tax Payment Receipt (Challan)]{.underline} within menu item
Withholding Tax Reports. The following options are available on the
screen enabling you to further customize your report.

-   **Cheque number:** Please enter the cheque number in this field.

-   **Due date:** Enter the due date of the challan in this field.

-   **Challan number:** Enter the challan number in this field.

-   **WHT amount:** This field displays the deducted WHT amount.

-   **Total amount:** This displays the total taxable amount.

-   **Supplier:** This shows the supplier for whom the deduction has
    been made.

-   **Bank name:** Enter a valid bank name in this field to specify the
    payment bank

-   **Cheque No. to be issued:** Enter the number of the cheque to be
    issued.

-   **Tax date:** Enter the due date for depositing the tax.
