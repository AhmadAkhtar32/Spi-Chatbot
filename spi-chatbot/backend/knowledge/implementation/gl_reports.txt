SPI General Ledger (GL) Module — REPORTS
Source: Sidat Hyder Financials Software Guide

### Reports 

The reports window has the following setups:

[Trial Balance:]{.underline}

As the title implies, this report prints the trial balance of a company.

[Getting there;]{.underline}

On the main menu screen go to the Reports menu and then select *Trial
Balance*.

This is one of the most useful reporting features that is available in
the GL system. Other than providing various options/criteria to generate
user requirements specific trial balances, the displayed report also has
a drill down feature enabling you to track transactions right down to
the individual voucher entry level.

Click the option that takes you to the printing screen. Some of the
options available to the user are discussed below for your convenience:

>  Make your selections to restrict the
> generated report to reflect only the Activity or the Opening Balances
> or the Closing Balance or a combination all three.
>
>  Make your choice to print Accounts
> that have balances without any transactions during the period or all
> accounts with balances and transactions entered for the period(s).
>
>  Make your choice to include only
> posted transactions or only unposted ones or if required print all
>
>  Choose to print only Debits or Credits columns or
> both or even include a net (balance) figures of both these figures
> including the option to skip printing of debit and credit figures and
> just displaying the net accumulative.
>
>  Choose to print the trial balance
> for the whole year, a number of years, a particular range of periods
> or dates or even a daily trial balance!
>
>  Make your choice to print the trial
> for the complete Chart of Accounts or just the Secondary Chart of
> Accounts or skip the group levels to print only detail level accounts
> for any given range of accounts or accounts

Specify levels, which means specify the structure level (of your Chart
of Accounts) for which you required the report. Choose to print for
level 1 or level 2 or level 3 as per the structure defined for your COA.
Upper levels automatically reflect lower level transactions and thus
limit the size of the report by reflecting detailed transactions ingroup
totals where group represents a level.

>  Choose whether you want the report
> to include or exclude yearend vouchers effects.
>
>  Choose to print row separators
> between separate account codes to improve readability
>
>  Option of the group level accounts
> is also available.
>
>  Print the report in any currency if
> the multi-currency option is installed.
>
> Print report in Currency A for transactions entered in currency B
>
>  If required, type pf currency can
> also be shown on the report by simply clicking on the check box.
>
>  Print the report in DOS mode or save
> the output in a text file
>
>  Define cost centers.
>
>  Accounts can be sorted out according
> to the code and the title.
>
>  Print for a range of sites or a
> consolidated trial balance for all sites
>
>  Use the Like option to print
> accounts matching a certain pattern. For example, to print accounts
> which have the third and fourth characters as 47 simply enter two
> underscores ('\_') then '47' followed by '%' sign (i.e.
>
> \_\_47%) in the account range specifications column

Go to the displayed report and double click any account code to view the
Ledger for that account code and in the ledger simply click to reach the
voucher level of that entry.

[Ledgers:]{.underline}

As the title implies, this options displays the ledger for the Chart of
Accounts.

[Getting there:]{.underline}

On the main menu screen go to the Reports menu and then select *Ledger*.

Click the option that takes you to the printing screen, which has
various criteria facilitating you to print the Ledger for any chart of
account, secondary chart of account or range of accounts.

>  If required then make your choice to
> limit the number of records to by specifying the account range, the
> parent a/c levels, the period range, cost centre, posting status, etc.
>
>  You are also given the choice print
> only group level account codes.
>
>  Make your selection to print the
> report in any desired reporting currencies provided the multi-currency
> option is installed.
>
>  Make your choice to have each
> account code on a separate page
>
>  If required specify to print even each cost centre
> on a separate page
>
>  Use the drill down feature to view
> the details of any entry by simply double clicking on the display to
> take you to the original voucher.
>
>  If you wish to calculate the opening
> balance, check the respective box and opening balance will be
> calculated.
>
>  Moreover, the status, opening
> /closing balance, project column, reference can also be shown by
> checking the respective boxes.
>
>  Print for a range of sites or a
> consolidated trial balance for all sites
>
>  Use the Like option to print
> accounts matching a certain pattern. For example, to print accounts
> which have the third and fourth characters as 47 simply enter two
> underscores ('\_') then '47' followed by '%' sign (i.e. \_\_47%) in
> the account range specifications column
>
>  For the purpose of discrete account
> information, click on " discrete a/c" and the information will be
> displayed along with the code and title of the account.
