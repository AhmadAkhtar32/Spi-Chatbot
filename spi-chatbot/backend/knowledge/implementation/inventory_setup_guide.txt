SPI Implementation Guide — Inventory Module Setup

1. Prerequisites
Before configuring the Inventory module, confirm the client's chart of
accounts has been finalized and the warehouse hierarchy (Site > Warehouse
> Bin Location) has been agreed upon during discovery. Inventory setup
cannot begin until the client has provided a finalized item master list
in the standard SPI import template.

2. Warehouse Hierarchy Configuration
Navigate to Inventory > Configuration > Warehouse Setup. Create Sites
first, then Warehouses under each Site, then Bin Locations under each
Warehouse. Bin Location codes must follow the format AA-00-00 (Aisle-
Rack-Shelf) to remain compatible with the barcode scanning module.

3. Item Master Import
Use Inventory > Configuration > Item Master Import to bulk-load items
from the client's finalized template. Common gap: clients often don't
have a consistent unit-of-measure (UOM) standard across items — this is
the single most frequent blocker during Inventory go-live and should be
flagged during discovery, not during import.

4. Reorder Point & Safety Stock Rules
Configure reorder points under Inventory > Configuration > Replenishment
Rules. If the client does not have historical consumption data, apply a
default safety stock of 15% above average monthly usage until at least
one full quarter of real data is available, then revisit.

5. Common Gap Analysis Findings
- Clients frequently expect automatic reorder-to-PO generation; this
  requires the Purchase module to also be licensed and configured —
  flag this dependency early if the client has only licensed Inventory.
- Multi-currency stock valuation is not supported out of the box and
  requires a custom valuation script — treat as a scoped change request,
  not standard configuration.
- Barcode label printing requires a separate hardware integration step,
  not covered in standard Inventory configuration; scope separately.

6. Go-Live Checklist
- Item master imported and validated (spot-check 10% of SKUs)
- Warehouse hierarchy matches client's physical site layout
- Reorder rules configured for at least top 20% of SKUs by usage volume
- At least one full cycle count completed in the test environment


### Frequently Asked Questions (Additional Test Coverage)

Q: What must be finalized before Inventory module configuration can begin?
A: The client's chart of accounts and warehouse hierarchy (Site > Warehouse > Bin Location), plus a finalized item master list in the standard import template.

Q: What format must Bin Location codes follow?
A: The format AA-00-00 (Aisle-Rack-Shelf), to remain compatible with the barcode scanning module.

Q: What default safety stock should be applied if there's no historical consumption data?
A: 15% above average monthly usage, until at least one full quarter of real data is available.

Q: Why might automatic reorder-to-PO generation not work even with Inventory configured correctly?
A: It requires the Purchase module to also be licensed and configured — this dependency should be flagged early.

Q: Is multi-currency stock valuation supported out of the box?
A: No — it requires a custom valuation script and should be treated as a scoped change request, not standard configuration.

Q: What should be checked on the Inventory go-live checklist?
A: Item master imported and spot-checked (10% of SKUs), warehouse hierarchy matching the physical site layout, reorder rules configured for the top 20% of SKUs by usage, and at least one full cycle count completed in test.
